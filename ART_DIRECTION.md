# Postscript: art direction and project-specific modeling rules

Project-specific companion to the generic skill guide `.claude/skills/blender-keytools/art_and_gotchas.md`.
Where they differ, this file wins for Postscript. Read once per chat before modeling for this game.

## Target look
- PS1-style horror in the spirit of Voices of the Void / KLETKA: low-poly but with real silhouettes, low-res
  point-filtered textures, few colours, small light radius, fog.
- Night, late autumn: wet, bare trees, fallen leaves. No snow.
- Dread without jump scares: nothing explained, everyday things that are slightly wrong.

## Style numbers
- Texel density now: walls ~64 px/m (256 px = 4 m x 2.6 m), floors ~107 px/m (256 px = 2.4 m), props 64 px textures.
  For a cruder PS1 look this could be halved (~32-50 px/m) with 16 colour levels per channel; text (door numbers,
  plates, signs) stays sharp. Key has not decided yet (see PROJECT_STATUS.md).
- Colour: 15-bit (5 bits per channel) on every painted texture (q15 in the painter scripts). Closest filtering
  in Blender, Point in Unity.
- The pixel look comes mostly from Unity: low internal render resolution (e.g. 480x270 render texture upscaled
  without filtering), Point filtering, optional PS1 vertex snapping / affine warping shader. Judge with
  `render_preview.py ... lowres`, smooth previews hide it.

## Palette and themes
- Every photo base is graded to a theme palette (tint by luminance, contrast 0.35-0.6). Current interior themes:
  Ochre (warm oil paint, terracotta/cream metlakh tiles, caged bulbs), Mint (hospital mint, terrazzo with brass
  strips, fluorescent tubes), Panel80 (chipboard wainscot, wallpaper, parquet-print linoleum, globe lamps),
  Decay (peeling blue paint over older green and concrete, debris, mould, emergency red light).
  v1 floors: green two-tone and blue/concrete (desaturated props, `_M` texture copies).
- Exterior: grey panel patchwork with dark seams, sodium orange street light, warm/curtained/TV-blue windows
  (~20% lit), dark distant blocks with sparse lit windows (not office-tower facade textures).

## Soviet panel-block details that sell it
- Stairwell paint: dark oil paint to ~1.5-1.6 m with a dark stripe, whitewash above; painted plinth.
- Floors: metlakh tiles, terrazzo, linoleum; cracked, patched, missing tiles.
- Doors: quilted dermatin with tacks, painted panel doors, veneer, steel; number plates, peepholes, bells with
  surface wiring; doormats (rag rugs, rubber).
- Surface-mounted cables with clips, electrical cabinets with meters, mailboxes with letters sticking out, fire
  hose cabinets, trash chute hatches, radiators under windows, notices with tear-off phone strips.
- Residents' tambours: wooden partitions or steel grilles with their own wallpaper and linoleum.
- Clutter: prams, sleds, boots, boxes, rolled carpets, buckets; apartments: wall unit with dishes, wall carpet,
  CRT TV, chandelier, oilcloth on the kitchen table, gas stove, tulle curtains.
- Yard: palisade fences, tyre planters, benches, playground, garage rows, trash area, parked old cars.

## Storytelling (small wrong things)
- A door painted over like the wall (no number, no handle), a repeated apartment number, a doormat in front of a
  blank wall, muddy footprints from the lift to the door that should not be there.
- Apartments: everything under sheets with the mirror covered and letters covering the floor (the player is a
  postman), furniture on the ceiling, a hallway that keeps going.
- Broken lift with doors ajar over an empty shaft, dead and flickering lamps, a lone chair facing something.
- Spaces that cannot fit: a post hall of numberless boxes, a queue of empty coats, a room inside itself, all the kitchens of
  the stairwell stacked without floors. Built from the same everyday props, only the arrangement or the scale is wrong.
- Characters stay readable as silhouettes at low resolution and should not look like people in costumes (Key's feedback):
  Mr. Nobody = coat + floating hat, Trader = floating triangle with a hole-eye and insect-thin arms with sleeve protectors
  (the only shop-clerk hint), Greed = red bell whose opening is an eyelid with an eye inside, spiky fur, money crown,
  Flower = a person-high stem with a face-sized bloom. Insides are pure black.

## Sizes used (1 unit = 1 m)
- Storey: ceiling 2.6 (interiors), 2.8 floor-to-floor on the 9-storey facade, 2.7 on the 5-storey; 3 m facade bays.
- Corridor 2 m wide; apartment door opening 0.94 x 2.07; interior door 0.8 x 2.0; window sill 0.85-0.9.
- Characters: Mr. Nobody 2.07 m with the hat, Trader triangle 1.27-1.82 m (hands at 0.92 m), Greed 2.9 m + crown, Flower 1.75 m.
- Lift cab inside 1.5 x 1.3 x 2.25 m (Trader version 2.2 x 2.6), telescopic doors 1.0 x 2.05, button plate 0.92-1.48 m.
- Lift doors 1.0 m wide in the v2 floors (real Soviet 320-500 kg lifts: ~0.7 m, dnaop.com; Key found 0.8 m too
  narrow in game).

## Repo conventions
- Levels are generated by scripts in `levels/_generator/` (kit.py shared helpers); rebuild from the generator,
  do not hand-edit generated .blend files. See PROJECT_STATUS.md for commands.
- Textures: external PNGs in `levels/textures/` with relative paths, every file listed in `SOURCES.md`.
  CC0 bases come from the 256 px previews of github.com/Papyszoo/CC0-Public-Domain-Textures
  (`texture_sheet.py` to pick, `paint_textures.src('am__Name')` to load).
- Lights in collection `Lights`, animated ones named *_Flicker; markers Spawn_Player / Exit_*.
