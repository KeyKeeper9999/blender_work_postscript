All bases are CC0 (ambientCG, cgbookcase), taken from the 256 px colour previews in
github.com/Papyszoo/CC0-Public-Domain-Textures, box-downscaled and cut to 15-bit colour (PS1 look).
Unity: Filter Mode = Point, Compression = None.
